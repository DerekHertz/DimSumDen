## Decisions (user-confirmed)

1. Horseshoe layout: back kiosks inward, front kiosks outward, each turned up to 30° toward the table. Pandas don't roam.
2. Pagoda kiosks: ink-tile roof, station-hue trim, noren sign, one paper lantern that lights only for a waiting panda. The brown sphere lanterns go.
3. Hues come from the `station-*` tokens. The `HUE` map in `Market.jsx` goes.
4. Looks: unique headgear per role, a scarf in the station hue, one held prop. The scout's lantern becomes a magnifier.
5. Lazy susan: two tiers, with the ready queue below (max 8, plus an overflow stack and pill) and claimed work above.
6. Tally: a wooden abacus. Its 5 h and Week rods carry plan usage, so the sidebar loses its usage meter. This replaced the stone stele on 09-30.
7. Cubs: a lidded hamper with a Pass-hue blanket.
8. Sidebar: Needs you, Stations (with glyphs and "coming online" pills), Queue. Overlays: zoom switcher (Den/Station/Panda/Workspace), intent bar, timeline.
9. Vocabulary: den words in anything a user sees. Workers are **pandas**. Code names don't change (`CONTEXT.md` "UI names").
10. Planned roles are drawn but not built: Drummer (Drum 鼓, on Bao), Librarian (Library 书, a scroll shelf beside the Tally), Painter (Front of House), Cub (hamper).
