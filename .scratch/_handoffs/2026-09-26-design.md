## Decisions made (all confirmed by the user)
- Plush everywhere: fuzzy fur shells, soft light, no ink outlines. The light "bamboo grove" theme is primary; dark is "lantern dusk".
- Bao sits upright at 11x scale, and cells perch on it: Brain on the crown, Muscles on the right shoulder and both knees. Heart is a drum on the left shoulder, Liver is a hotpot on the grass, Memory is a pavilion, Stem is a basket, Immune patrols the path.
- Cells are about 1/10 of Bao. There are no mats; each cell gets a contact shadow. Qi ribbons run only between live organs and never cross the face.
- Dormant organs are faded 45% toward pearl, their cells doze with fur hidden, and they carry a dashed "coming online" label.
- Seven cell types, each with a hat and prop: orchestrator (Abbot), product (Storyteller), architect (Scholar), developer (Maker), scout (Wanderer), security (Gatekeeper), qa (Taster). Security and QA have designs but no genomes; the user will define their roles in a separate session.
- Level 2 has a right panel. Level 3 is a front three-quarter shot (not over the shoulder), with the cell feed on the left, the cell card on the right, and the tablet mini-terminal mapped onto the 3D screen. Level 4 is IDE-style inside the tablet bezel on light paper surfaces.
- There is one bottom intent bar at every level, scoped to the organism or the current cell.
- The user wants to be asked when a direction is unclear, as selectable options (AskUserQuestion).

## Gotchas
- Plush origin is the model's centre, so place cells at surface + `CELL_SCALE`. Placing them at surface height sinks them into Bao.
- Blender MCP calls time out on renders longer than about 30 s. Queue them with `bpy.app.timers.register` and poll for the output file.
- `make_dormant` twins must never be re-toonified; `toon.py` skips `·dormant`. Fur shell materials lose alpha if faded, so hide the shells on dormant cells instead.
- The `Screen` material is shared, so darkening it for Level 3 turned every tablet dark. Give it a per-cell copy if that matters.
- Close-ups show fur grain. For Level 3, Bao's strand noise was raised to 9000 and fstop set to 1.6.
- In the Design canvas, re-read `canvas.json` and each artboard before publishing; the user edits live.
