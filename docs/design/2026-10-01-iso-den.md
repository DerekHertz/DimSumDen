# Isometric den: numbers from the frame

Every den-iso-v1 cell reads this page instead of the canvas. Source: the "Level 1 · Den (isometric, floating cards, dev-03 selected)" artboard (`Level1-Den-Iso.dc.html`, https://claude.ai/artifact/AFEGyVKV5s6GbTFraA5U3G), read against the old "Level 1 · Den" artboard. The frame is hand-drawn SVG at 1440 x 900: its pixels are a target, not a 3D spec. Where the two disagree, this page says which wins. Layout rules of [den-map.md](den-map.md) stand unless a row below says otherwise.

Axes as in den-map: +x right, +z toward the camera, y up. Items marked **(D1, D2, D3)** are decisions for the user; see the last section.

## 1. Projection

True orthographic camera. The frame's kiosk edges run at 30 degrees from horizontal (slope 0.577) and its row spacing implies the same pitch; its stone ring (2:1) is the one outlier, drawn flat by hand.

| Number | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Heading (yaw) | 0: looks along -z, as today's camera | The frame's composition is left-right symmetric, like den-map's horseshoe. A 45 degree heading would turn the whole layout into a diagonal; the frame does not show that. |
| Pitch | atan(1/sqrt 2) = 35.264 degrees (sin p = 0.57735, cos p = 0.81650) | True isometric. Back and front kiosk rows land 208 px apart at 1440 x 900, the frame shows 212. |
| Look-at target | world (0, 0, -2.4), Bao's feet | Screen anchor (W/2, 0.52 H), the frame's ring centre (720, 470). |
| Pixels per world unit `k` | `min(W / (W < 600 ? 13.0 : 16.4), H / 10.2)`: 87.8 at 1440 x 900, 28.8 at 375 x 667 | 87.8 reproduces the frame to within 12 px on Steamers, Front of House and the hamper. The phone rule fits the widest kiosk row (12.4 units) plus 0.6. |
| Zoom | dolly factor `d` in [0.55, 1.2], default 1; `k = k_default / d`: 73 to 160 px/unit at 1440 x 900 | Same range and semantics as `ZOOM_MIN`/`ZOOM_MAX` today. Wheel `d += deltaY * 0.001`; keys + and - step 0.1. |
| Camera rig | position `target + 40 * (0, sin p, cos p)`, up (0, 1, 0), near 0.1, far 120; frustum half-width `W / 2k`, half-height `H / 2k` | Square pixels; distance only has to clear the scene. |

World to screen (target `t`, screen centre `c = (W/2, 0.52 H)`):

```
sx = cx + k * (x - tx)
sy = cy + k * ((z - tz) * sin p - (y - ty) * cos p)
ground pick (y = 0):  x = tx + (sx - cx) / k,   z = tz + (sy - cy) / (k * sin p)
```

Pan. Drag moves the target on the ground: `dtx = -dx / k`, `dtz = -dy / (k sin p)`. Arrow keys step 0.5 in x as today.
- x: `|tx| <= max(0, WIDEST_STALL_EDGE - W / 2k)`, the rule used today with the new half-width.
- z: `|tz + 2.4| <= max(0, 3.9 - H / 2k) / sin p`. At the default there is no vertical pan; at `d = 0.55` it is about 1.1 units of screen height, enough to reach Bao's crown or the front row.

Default frame (target above, `d = 1`). Every kiosk, Bao, the susan, the hamper, the Tally and both pads fall inside the viewport at both sizes.

States.

## 7. Accessibility and words

- Contrast (light, measured): `alarm` on `alarm-zone` 4.59; `alarm` on `surface-200` 5.73; `ink-muted` on `surface-200`, `surface-100`, `surface-000` 6.32, 5.81, 5.27; `lantern` on `surface-200` 5.50; `qi` on `surface-200` 6.10; `surface-200` on `qi-fill` 5.05; `on-lantern` on `lantern-fill` 9.93; `surface-200` on the four station hues 6.15 to 6.80; `line-strong` borders on `surface-200` 3.82 and on the meter track 3.18 (control border minimum 3). Dark values come from the same tokens; re-measure in review.
- Status is never colour alone: each state carries its icon and text. Priority is "P0" text, never a hue.
- Keyboard: every control is a button or link. Tab order: left card, Needs you, Stations & queue, scene chips as today, zoom switcher, intent bar, timeline. Focus ring: 2 px `focus-ring`, 2 px offset. `a` and `d` act on the top gate only while Needs you is open and focus is not in a text field. Scene keys stay: arrows pan, + and - zoom.
- Landmarks and names: `section aria-label="dev-03, selected panda"`; `aria-label="Needs you, 3 waiting"`; `nav aria-label="Zoom level"`; the meter keeps `role="meter"` with its label. A new failed entry is announced politely ("dev-03 failed · 3 tests"). The scene SVG or canvas label lists the stations, Bao and the dormant pads in den words.
- Touch (`pointer: coarse`): controls at least 44 px (the frame's 30 and 32 px buttons grow).
- Reduced motion: section 4 and 5. No parallax or sway added.
- Den words: panda, role, station, Needs you, order, susan, Tally, Cubs, Live. None of organism, organ, cell, genome, apoptosis, endocrine in a visible label or `aria-label`. The frame breaks this in three places, do not copy them: the scene label ("cell pandas", "Pass cells"), the id chip `cell/dev-03/T-117`, and the `[TICKET]` placeholders. Show the real ticket id.
- Screen-space chips and badges are not tab stops unless they act (a badge is not a control).

## 8. For the user's sign-off (gate)

Nothing here ships until the user approves it.
1. **Tokens that ship in code:** G1 to G6 (G7 skipped). Each needs the design system's `tokens.json` updated first; the designer publishes that after the user's yes.
2. **D2, nearest-token substitutions:** ground `grove-grass`, bamboo `grove-mid`, `grove-hill`, `grove-near`, roof `panda-ink`.
3. **D1, kiosk yaw:** the frame draws all four kiosks the same (corner forward, facing lower left, about -0.8 rad). Digest keeps den-map's mirrored +-0.52 (left kiosks face the table). The frame is closer to a single reused drawing than a decision; say so if you want it literal.
4. **D3, kiosk click selects the most urgent panda** (a rule the frame does not show).
5. **Camera heading 0, pitch 35.264 degrees** (section 1): the alternative, a 45 degree heading, would re-lay every position.
6. Moves beyond the frame's own: Steamers and Front of House 0.3 inward to +-3.0, Tally yaw to 0. Everything else keeps den-map.
